FROM A 1999 EXECUTABLE

A lost game,
found in the code.

Pimp Wyld began as a compact DOS-era trading and territory game. The original source disappeared, but the surviving PKLITE-packed Borland executable still held its rules, tables, menus, and attitude.

Version 3.0 is a clean-room reconstruction—not the lost source. It keeps the ten commodities, ten weapons, cash, bank, debt, health, travel, riots, and status systems, then expands them into a 60-day Big Ten campaign with turn-based combat, modern controls, and new events.
