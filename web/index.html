BUILT IN THE 1990s · REBUILT TODAY

A kid's idea,
finally realized.

Pimp Wyld started in the 1990s, built by a kid who wanted to make his own drug-lord game. It was rough, strange, and unmistakably a product of its time—a homemade DOS experiment with trading, travel, territory, and attitude.

Today, we rebuilt it into what it was intended to be. The original's weird high-school-and-younger setting is gone, replaced by a fictionalized modern college experience spanning 18 Big Ten campuses. Version 3.0 keeps the core systems, then expands them into a 60-day campaign with turn-based combat, modern controls, and new events.

THIS IS A FICTIONAL SIMULATION. It is satire and entertainment, not an endorsement of drugs, violence, exploitation, or illegal activity. Nothing here should be taken seriously or copied in real life.
